Streaming Timeouts

expand_more

Industry-standard timeouts based on RFC 8216 (HLS), Apple HLS Best Practices, and Google Media CDN guidelines. Lower values = faster failover but may cause false failures on slow networks.

Connection Phase

Time to establish TCP connection. Industry standard: 1-5s. Default: 5
Time to receive first data after connection. Detects "connected but no data" scenarios. Default: 5

Streaming Phase

Time without data before reconnecting. Industry standard: 10-20s. Default: 10

Failover Phase

Total time to try all providers before showing error. Industry standard: 5-15s. Default: 15
Time a failing provider is skipped. Lower = faster recovery. Default: 30

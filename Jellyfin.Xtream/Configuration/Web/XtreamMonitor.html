info Understanding the Dashboard

Active Streams

Number of currently playing streams. High utilization may affect quality.

Errors

Total transmission errors. Types: Packet (data loss), Continuity (frame drops), Sync (timing issues).

A/V Drift (ms)

Audio/Video synchronization offset. <40ms is ideal, >100ms may cause visible desync.

Throughput (MB/s)

Data transfer rate. Higher values indicate better stream quality and bitrate.
